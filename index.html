The Shared Technical Roots

Financial concentration is only half the picture. The ecosystem relies heavily on a few core libraries and SDKs maintained by a small group of Long-Term Support (LTS) developers. When downstream apps rely on these libraries, the maintainer's true Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) for network control is significantly higher than their grant amount suggests.

Funding Deployment Timeline

December 2022
14.17 BTC Fiatjaf Fund

Jack Dorsey deploys 14.17 BTC directly to Fiatjaf following a Twitter exchange. This acts as an initial catalyst for dedicated Nostr development.

May 2023
#startsmall $10M Grant (via OpenSats)

Jack Dorsey announces a $10M donation to OpenSats, explicitly earmarking half ($5M) to advance the Nostr protocol. A committee of Nostr developers is established.

2024
LTS Grants Formalized & Concentration Sets

OpenSats begins issuing Long-Term Support (LTS) grants, formally supporting repeat-funders (Damus, Coracle, Soapbox, Amethyst). This establishes the HHI concentration base. By the end of 2024, ~$8.9M is allocated.

2025
Payout Consolidation

$3.2M in payouts mapped. Approximately 35% of the Nostr Fund allocation is directed toward LTS maintainers, highlighting the ecosystem's reliance on a concentrated infrastructure core.

2026 (Mapped Future)
Ongoing Payable Structure

H1 2026 shows $1.43M in payouts. June 2026 currently holds $801K in mapped payables.
