Funding Deployment & Governance Timeline

Tracing the flow of capital alongside the structural evolution of the OpenSats board. The data highlights a pattern of overlapping industry roles, the use of abstracted LLCs for board seats, and critical unresolved governance gaps.

2020–2022: Formation
Early Board & 14.17 BTC Catalyst

OpenSats is founded. Ben Price and Lisa Neigut are listed as directors. In Dec 2022, Jack Dorsey deploys 14.17 BTC directly to Fiatjaf, sparking dedicated Nostr development.

2023: The First Wave
$10M Grant & Board Expansion

Jack Dorsey gifts $10M ($5M to Nostr). NVK and Gigi join the board. First major Nostr grant wave hits in July. By September, minutes reveal discussions about "setting up LLCs for board members."

2024: LLCs & LTS Formalization
$21M Grant & Structural Obfuscation

In March, the board unanimously approves 9 LLCs (Freedom Tech LLC, Orange Coin Good LLC, etc.) as board members. May brings another $21M #startsmall gift ($5M to Nostr). Long-Term Support (LTS) rolls out.

2025: Consolidation & Recusals
92.3% LTS Retention

Nostr LTS retains 12 of 13 people. In September, an unidentified board member auto-recuses from ALL Nostr applications, dropping the approval threshold to 4/8. The reason and identity remain unknown.

2026: Restructuring
Return to "Natural Persons"

Following 3,000+ application evaluations, James O'Beirne resigns in June while his grant plan is discussed. The board votes to restructure back to "natural persons". NVK's tenure ends in August.
